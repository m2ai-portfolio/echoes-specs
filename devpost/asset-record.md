# Echoes: asset and license record

Release candidate: **Echoes** (lens name in `Echoes.esproj`), Signal Arch v1. Recorded 2026-11-04 at Increment 4. This record covers what is in the lens and the repository. It doesn't grant any license. Third-party components stay under their own terms.

## Summary

- The task, geometry, text, and code are original work made for this project.
- The project starts from Snap's **SPECS Base Template** and uses four Snap packages from the Lens Studio Asset Library.
- **No third-party music, sound, imagery, fonts beyond Lens Studio's default, 3D models, or branding are used.** The lens has no audio at all. It uses no commercial brick brand, set, or likeness. "Signal Arch" is an invented generic model.

## Disclosures that go with every presentation of the lens

- **Virtual validation only.** Echoes checks placements against authored task data in a virtual scene. It doesn't use the camera, doesn't recognize physical bricks, and doesn't inspect a real model. The lens shows `Virtual build demo · enlarged pieces · no physical bricks are recognized` at all times. On completion it shows `Virtual check only: no physical model was inspected.`
- **Enlarged geometry.** Pieces are deliberately drawn much larger than real construction bricks so they can be seen and selected in Specs. The finished arch is about 30 × 18.5 × 14 cm on a 30 × 2 × 14 cm base: pillars 5 × 8 × 5 cm, beam 26 × 3 × 5 cm, pedestal 6 × 2.5 × 6 cm, marker 5 × 3 × 6 cm (`Assets/Echoes/Scripts/SignalArchTask.ts`). It doesn't represent true-to-life brick scale.
- **Authored help.** Help, instructions, and corrections are fixed text written for this task. They are not generated by a live model, and the lens makes no network calls.
- **Tested in Preview only.** No Specs device was used. Comfort, real hand tracking, and readability on the real display are unverified.

## Not used

No audio, music, or voice. No third-party images, video, icons, or 3D assets. No external fonts (all text uses Lens Studio's default font). Echoes code doesn't read the camera feed and uses no location or network APIs (the template's `Device Camera Texture` asset is part of its render setup, not read by Echoes). No live model or remote service. No personal data is collected, recorded, or followed.
